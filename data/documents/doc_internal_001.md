**Classification:** INTERNAL ONLY | **Document ID:** doc_internal_001

## 1. Scope and Objective
This standard operating procedure is restricted to full-time employees, authorized contractors, and internal staff. The operational guidelines described herein govern day-to-day engineering, security configurations, and facilities access.

## 2. Operational Procedures and Governance
All systems must adhere to strict zero-trust network policies. Access keys must be rotated every 90 days, and production deploys require two peer reviews. For urgent escalations or system failures, staff must follow the internal on-call protocol.
